# Apple Card Studies: assets and dependencies

These seven editorial studies use bundled media and relative files; no network request is needed to render them. Keep `apple/` and `lib/` alongside the HTML pages.

The photographs are Apple product imagery used for design study. Apple retains its imagery and trademark rights; the repository's MIT license does not relicense these third-party assets. This is an independent educational example, not an Apple product or endorsement. Replace media with appropriately licensed project assets for production. Displayed figures are composition examples, not product specifications.

Images have neutral filenames and no retained EXIF, comments or source-machine metadata. Public source pages are recorded below; they are attribution links, not runtime dependencies.
